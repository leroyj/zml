# DFlash2 decode benchmark prompts: one per line, `name<TAB>prompt`.
# Long answers on purpose, so tok/s and acceptance stats are stable.
code	Implement a thread-safe LRU cache in Python as a reusable module. Requirements: a generic `LRUCache[K, V]` class with `get`, `put`, `delete`, `__len__`, `__contains__` and iteration from most to least recently used; optional per-entry time-to-live with lazy expiry; a maximum size in entries and an optional maximum total weight computed by a user-supplied function; eviction callbacks; and hit/miss/eviction statistics. Use a doubly linked list plus a dict, not OrderedDict. Include full type hints and docstrings, then write a thorough pytest test suite covering eviction order, TTL expiry, weight limits, callbacks and concurrent access from multiple threads. Finish with a short explanation of the time complexity of each operation.
review	You are reviewing a design document. The service ingests sensor readings from about forty thousand devices, each sending one JSON message every ten seconds over MQTT. Messages are validated, enriched with device metadata from a PostgreSQL table, and written to a time-series database. A nightly batch job aggregates readings into hourly and daily rollups, and a dashboard queries those rollups. Recently the team observed that ingestion latency spikes every night at around two in the morning, that some messages are silently dropped when the metadata table is being vacuumed, and that the dashboard occasionally shows gaps for devices that reconnect after a network outage. Write a detailed review of roughly one thousand words: for each of the three problems, explain the most likely root causes, how to confirm each hypothesis with metrics or experiments, and concrete fixes with their trade-offs. End with a prioritized action plan and a list of monitoring alerts the team should add.
story	Write a short story of about one thousand words set in a lighthouse on a remote northern island during the winter of 1911. The keeper, a retired ship's engineer, finds a sealed brass cylinder washed up on the rocks. Tell the story in the first person, with vivid sensory detail about the weather, the machinery of the lamp and the isolation, include at least two flashbacks to his years at sea, some dialogue with the supply boat captain, and an ending that is quiet but surprising.
math	Solve the following problems step by step, showing all intermediate work and checking each answer at the end. 1) Find all real solutions of x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 = 0. 2) A tank fills through pipe A in 6 hours and through pipe B in 9 hours, while pipe C drains it in 12 hours; starting empty with all three open, how long until it is full? 3) Compute the sum of the first 50 terms of the arithmetic sequence 7, 11, 15, ... 4) Prove that the sum of the first n odd numbers is n^2 by induction. 5) Find the derivative of f(x) = x^3 ln(x) and the equation of the tangent line at x = e.
table	Produce a Markdown reference table of the 30 most commonly used Linux command-line tools. Columns: command, category, one-line description, a typical example invocation, and one common pitfall. After the table, give a numbered list of ten one-liners that combine several of these tools with pipes, each followed by a one-sentence explanation.
